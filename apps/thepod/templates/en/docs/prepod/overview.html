prePod

Your podcast planning hub. Before you record, you need to define your show's identity, plan the content, coordinate your team and guests, and know the order of every segment. prePod puts all of that in one place.

01 What can you do?

IDENTITY

Branding

Define your podcast's visual identity: name, tagline, colors, logo and cover art. Everything flows automatically into prodPod and your public landing page.

MANAGEMENT

Episodes and Kanban

Manage your episodes on a visual board with six columns: Idea, Planning, With script, Recorded, Published and Archived. Drag to change the status.

STRUCTURE

Rundown and script

Build the structure of each episode with blocks (Intro, Segment, Interview, Sponsor, Break, Outro) and write the detailed script with speakers and timings.

TEAM

Guests and RSVP

Add guests, assign roles (Host, Co-host, Guest) and send invitations by email or link. Each guest confirms with a single click.

SCHEDULE

Calendar

Schedule episodes with a date and time. See everything on a monthly calendar, drag to reschedule and download .ics files for your own calendar app.

MONETIZATION

Sponsors

Register advertisers, link them to rundown blocks and generate reports with minutes and episodes per sponsor. Export CSV for your clients.

REUSE

Templates

Create reusable structures (rundown + base script) for episodes with a recurring format. Save time on every new episode.

PUBLISH

Landing page and RSS

Publish a web page with your podcast's info, episode list, team and an RSS feed compatible with every podcast platform.

02 Who is it for?

BASIC

Independent host

You record alone or with the occasional guest. You use prePod to plan your content, keep a clear rundown and publish your landing page with RSS.

INTERMEDIATE

Small team (2-4)

A host, a co-host and maybe a producer. You use the kanban to coordinate episodes, the script to split up who says what, and RSVP for outside guests.

ADVANCED

Studio with several shows

Several podcasts with different teams. Each podcast has its own branding, sponsors, landing page and reports. Admins manage users and permissions.
