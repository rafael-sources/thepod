KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS

Hotkeys and mouse gestures

Shortcuts to work faster in prePod without relying on the mouse.

01 Keyboard

KeyAction
Ctrl+N
 Go to the episode board
Ctrl+Enter
 Submit idea in the Ideas panel
Esc
 Close the active modal
NOTE

Keyboard shortcuts only work when your cursor isn't inside a text field.

02 Kanban

GestureAction
Drag an episode between columns
 Change the status of the episode
Up/down arrows on a card
 Change the position on the public landing page

03 Calendar

GestureAction
Click an empty day
 Create an episode on that date
Click an episode
 Open the detail view
Drag an episode to another day
 Reschedule (the time is kept)

04 Rundown

GestureAction
Drag a block by its handle
 Reorder blocks

05 Script

GestureAction
Up/down arrows on a section
 Reorder sections of the script
