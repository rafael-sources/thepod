CONCEPTS

The prePod vocabulary

The key terms you need to know to get the most out of prePod.

01 Podcast

Your show. Each podcast has its own branding (name, colors, logo, cover art), episodes, sounds, templates, sponsors and public landing page. You can have multiple podcasts in one account, each fully independent.

02 Episode

A single edition of your podcast. Each episode moves across the kanban board through six states:

Idea → Planning → With script → Recorded → Published → Archived

An episode contains: rundown, script, guest roster, scheduled date and distribution links. When it moves to "Published", the show notes are generated automatically.

03 Kanban

The visual board where you manage your episodes. The six columns represent the production stages (the Archived column is hidden by default — turn it on from the column picker). You drag episode cards between columns to reflect their progress. Each card shows the episode number, title, scheduled date and estimated duration.

04 Rundown

The skeletal structure of an episode. It's an ordered sequence of blocks, where each block has a type, a title, an estimated duration and notes.

TypeTypical use
IntroEpisode opening
SegmentContent section
InterviewQuestions and answers
SponsorAd slot
BreakCut or transition
OutroEpisode closing
OtherAny block that doesn't fit the categories above

Think of the rundown as the skeleton: it defines the order and timings, but not the detailed content.

05 Script

The detailed content of each episode. More granular than the rundown: each section has a speaker (who talks), the text or notes of what's said, technical cues and an estimated duration in seconds. The script complements the rundown with the fine detail.

06 Template

A reusable structure that combines a base rundown and a base script. When you create a new episode, you can pick a template to pre-fill the structure. Ideal if your podcast has a recurring format (for example, you always open with the same intro, then three segments and a closing).

You can mark a template as default so it's applied automatically to every new episode.

07 Roster (Guests)

The list of participants in an episode. Each guest has:

  • Name and email (optional).
  • Role: Host, Co-host or Guest.
  • RSVP status: Pending, Yes, No or Maybe.
  • Bio: a short description of the guest.

Guests don't need a thePod account. They get an RSVP link and confirm with one click. When you send the episode to prodPod, the full roster goes along with it.

08 Sponsors

Advertisers tied to your podcast. Each sponsor has a name, URL, ad-read text, notes and an active/paused status. You link them to "Sponsor" blocks in the rundown.

prePod keeps track of how many episodes and minutes each sponsor has had, and you can export a CSV report to share with the advertiser.

09 Public landing page

A web page for your podcast that anyone can visit without logging in. It includes:

  • Podcast info (name, description, cover art).
  • List of published episodes.
  • Team section (photos, names, roles, social links).
  • Custom content with a visual editor.
  • RSS feed compatible with Apple Podcasts, Spotify and other platforms.

Three design templates are available: Minimal, Bold and Classic.

10 Show notes

Text generated automatically when an episode moves to the "Published" state. It contains the episode chapters (derived from the rundown blocks) in a YouTube-compatible format. You can copy the full text or just the chapters with one click.
