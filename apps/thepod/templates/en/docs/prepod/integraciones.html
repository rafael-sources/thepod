INTEGRATIONS

How prePod connects with the suite

prePod is the first layer: everything you plan here flows to the other two apps automatically.

01 prePod to prodPod

When you click Send to prodPod on an episode, a recording session is created automatically with:

  • Guest roster: names, roles and emails so you can invite them straight from prodPod.
  • Soundboard: every sound you set up in the podcast's library, ready to use during the recording.
  • Planned markers: the rundown blocks become chapter markers the host can trigger during the recording.

Once the recording is underway, the episode in prePod shows the status in real time: "recording now" or "recording ready".

TIP

Sending the same episode again updates the existing session — it doesn't create a new one. Handy if you added a guest at the last minute.

02 prePod to postPod

When you click Send to postPod, an editing project linked to the episode is created. If the episode was already recorded in prodPod, the recordings are already available in the project.

Along with the episode go the branding (logo, background, cover), the sponsor logos, the soundboard sounds, the guest photos and the rundown with its planned offsets. If you send the episode again, the project in postPod is refreshed with the updated data without touching the edit. The details are in the postPod doc.

03 Calendar and .ics

You can download the recording schedule as an .ics file:

  • Per episode: "Download .ics" button in the episode detail view.
  • Whole podcast: download button in the Calendar view.

The files are compatible with Google Calendar, Outlook and Apple Calendar.

NOTE

The .ics is a one-time download. If you change an episode's date, download the file again to update your external calendar.

04 Email invitations

If your instance has email configured, RSVP invitations are sent straight to the guest's inbox with an .ics file attached. If it isn't configured, you can copy the invitation link and send it manually by any other means (WhatsApp, email, etc.).

The guest confirms with a single click, no thePod account needed.

05 Public landing page and RSS

Your podcast's public landing page can be visited without logging in. It includes:

  • Episode list (if you turned it on).
  • Team section.
  • Custom content (text blocks, images, etc.).
  • RSS feed compatible with Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Google Podcasts and other podcast directories.

06 Social networks

In Podcast settings → Social you connect YouTube, Instagram and TikTok (and the feed for Spotify and Apple Podcasts). prePod stores the permission to publish on your behalf, never your password. Nothing is published from there: that's done by pubPod, in thePod for Mac, with what comes out of postPod. Instagram requires a professional account; TikTok leaves the video in your inbox so you can publish it from your phone; YouTube stays private until Google verifies the app.
