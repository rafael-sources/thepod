GETTING STARTED

Your first podcast in prePod

From zero to an episode ready to record in under ten minutes. No technical knowledge needed — just a name and an idea.

01 Open prePod

Open thePod in your browser and sign in. You use the same credentials for all four apps (prePod, prodPod, postPod, amaPod). From the hub, click the prePod card.

02 Create your first podcast

On the main screen you'll see your podcasts as visual cards. Click + New podcast and type the name of your show. It's created with default values you can customize.

03 Set up the branding

When you open the podcast, the first tab is Branding. This is where you define your show's identity:

  • Identity: name, tagline and description.
  • Colors: five pickers (primary, secondary, accent, background and text) with hex input.
  • Typography: heading font, body font and text alignment.
  • Assets: upload your logo (up to 500x500) and cover art (up to 1200x800).

In the right column you'll see a live preview that reflects your changes instantly.

TIP

You don't need to fill everything in now. You can come back to Branding any time. Changes are saved automatically.

04 Create your first episode

Go to the Episodes tab. You'll see a kanban board with six columns. Click + New episode in whichever column you prefer (for example, "Planning") and fill in:

  • Episode number: suggested automatically.
  • Title: the name of the episode.
  • Template (optional): if you've already created one, select it to pre-fill the rundown and script.

The episode shows up as a card you can drag between columns as it progresses.

05 Build the rundown

Click the episode card to open its detail view. In the Rundown section, add blocks to define the structure:

  • Intro: the episode opening.
  • Segment: a content section.
  • Interview: a Q&A block.
  • Sponsor: an ad slot (you can link a registered sponsor).
  • Break: a cut or transition.
  • Outro: the episode closing.

Each block has a title, an estimated duration and notes. Drag the six-dot handle to reorder. At the bottom you'll see the total estimated duration.

06 Write the script

In the Script section, add more detailed sections:

  • Speaker: who talks in this section.
  • Content: the text or the speaker's notes.
  • Notes: technical cues or context.
  • Duration: estimated time in seconds.

The script saves automatically every time you stop typing. Use the arrows to reorder sections.

07 Add guests

In the Guests (Roster) panel, click "Add guest" and fill in:

  • Name: the guest's name.
  • Email (optional): to send the invitation by email.
  • Role: Host, Co-host or Guest.

To confirm attendance, you have two options:

  • Click "Invite" to send an email with the RSVP link.
  • Copy the invitation link and send it however you like (WhatsApp, manual email, etc.).

The guest confirms with a single click, no thePod account needed.

08 Schedule the date

In the episode detail view, set the recording date and time. You can also use the Calendar tab to see all your episodes on a monthly calendar and drag them between days to reschedule.

Download an .ics file for a single episode or for the whole calendar to import it into Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar.

09 Send to prodPod

When everything is ready to record, click Send to prodPod in the episode's integration bar. This automatically creates a recording session in prodPod with:

  • The guest roster (names, roles and emails for the invitations).
  • Your full soundboard.
  • The planned chapter markers (from the rundown blocks).

From that point on, prePod shows you the recording status: "recording now" while the session is active, or "recording ready" when it's done.
