amaPod

Questions from your audience, live and in order. amaPod runs Q&A (AMA) events where viewers send in questions, a moderator builds the queue, and each participant goes on air by video when it's their turn — integrated with the prodPod studio.

01 What can you do?

EVENTS

Q&A events

Create an event with a title, description and chat mode. Activate it when the broadcast starts and end it when you're done. Reuse formats with templates.

MODERATION

Question queue

Participants sign up with their name and up to 3 questions. You decide who gets into the queue, in what order, and who goes on air.

FRICTIONLESS

Participants without an account

The audience joins with a simple link — no need to sign up for thePod. Each participant gets their own access token.

FLOW

Pre-connection

The next 3 in the queue pre-connect to the room before going on air: camera and microphone ready, zero wait between questions.

COMMUNITY

Chat and viewers

Open, moderated or off chat. Viewers follow the event from a public page and can give likes.

ARCHIVE

Recording

Record the event as an MP4 file on the server. When it ends, it's exported automatically to postPod for editing.

02 Who is it for?

CREATORS

Podcasters with an active audience

Turn your listeners into the stars: an AMA episode where the audience asks on video, in order and without chaos.

EVENTS

Launches and live sessions

Presentations, webinars or post-event Q&A rounds with full moderation and automatic recording.

PRODUCTION

Shows in prodPod

The amaPod panel is embedded inside the prodPod studio: the host sees the queue and puts questions on air without switching apps.
