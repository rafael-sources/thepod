INTEGRATIONS

How amaPod connects with the suite

amaPod is the audience layer: it brings the public's questions into the studio and hands the recording over to editing.

01 Inside prodPod

An amaPod event can be linked to a prodPod recording session. When that happens, the studio shows an embedded amaPod — Q&A panel: the host sees the queue of approved questions and puts participants on air without leaving prodPod.

Technically, prodPod embeds the event's /embed/<id> view — full moderation is still available in amaPod for a dedicated moderator.

02 To postPod

If the event was recorded, when you end it the MP4 recording is exported automatically to postPod as an editing project. From there you can cut out the best moments, generate captions with Whisper and export vertical clips for social networks.

03 Accounts and access

  • Host / moderator: uses the same thePod account as in the other apps (shared session).
  • Participants: come in with the public link and their own access token — no account.
  • Viewers: the viewing page is public, no login.

04 What about prePod?

For now, amaPod events are created directly in amaPod — they aren't planned from prePod. If your Q&A is part of an episode, plan the episode in prePod as always and create the amaPod event separately, linking it to the prodPod session.
