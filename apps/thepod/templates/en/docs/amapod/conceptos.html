CONCEPTS

The amaPod vocabulary

The key terms for understanding how a live Q&A event works.

01 Event

A question-and-answer session. It goes through three states:

Created → Active → Ended

  • Created: in preparation. The audience can't participate yet. It can only be deleted in this state.
  • Active: the video room is open, the queue works in real time.
  • Ended: the room has closed. If there was a recording, it travels to postPod.

02 Participant

Someone in the audience who wants to ask a question. They sign up with their name from the public link — no account, no sign-up. They get their own access token that identifies their place in the queue.

When signing up they can write up to 3 pre-questions so the moderator knows what they want to talk about.

03 The queue

The heart of amaPod. Each participant moves through these states:

Pending → In queue → On air → Handled

  • Pending: asked to participate, waiting for the moderator's approval.
  • In queue: approved, with a position number. Positions are renumbered automatically.
  • On air: connected to the video room, asking their question live.
  • Handled: their turn is over.

A participant can also end up rejected (the moderator discarded their request) or withdrawn (they left before their turn).

04 Pre-connection

The first 3 in the queue connect to the room ahead of time: they choose their camera and microphone and wait on standby, invisible to the public. When the moderator gives them the air, they appear instantly. This gets rid of the usual dead time of “let's see… can you hear me?”.

05 Censor

An emergency button for the moderator: it mutes and hides the participant who's on air immediately, without closing the event. Useful if someone uses the air for something inappropriate.

06 Chat modes

  • Open: all messages are posted instantly.
  • Moderated: messages from the audience need the host's approval before they're visible.
  • Off: there's no chat.

07 Viewer

Someone watching the event with no intention of asking. They come in through the event's public page, without an account, and can give likes. If chat is on, they can write too.

08 Video room

The event's live video call (WebRTC technology with LiveKit). It holds up to 10 simultaneous participants: the host, whoever is on air and the pre-connected ones. The general audience doesn't take up a spot in the room — they watch from the viewers' page.

09 Event template

A reusable format: title, description, chat mode and branding saved to create recurring events in one click (for example, “Friday AMA”).
