GETTING STARTED

Your first Q&A event

From zero to a live event with a moderated question queue. Your audience only needs a link.

01 Go into amaPod

Open thePod in your browser and sign in — the same credentials for all four apps (prePod, prodPod, postPod, amaPod). From the hub, click the amaPod card. Only the host needs an account: participants and viewers come in through public links.

02 Create the event

Click + New event and fill in:

  • Title and description: what the audience will see when they come in.
  • Chat mode: open, moderated (messages need your approval) or off.
  • Template (optional): if you saved a previous event's format, use it to pre-fill everything.

The event starts in the Created state — it isn't visible as a broadcast yet.

03 Share the links

  • Participation link: whoever opens it can sign up with their name and write up to 3 questions. No sign-up.
  • Viewer link: a public page to follow the event live and give likes.

04 Activate the event

When you're ready to start, click Activate. The live video room is created and the event moves to the Active state. From here on, the queue works in real time.

05 Moderate the queue

Requests from the audience arrive as pending. For each one you can:

  • Approve: the participant enters the queue with a position number.
  • Reject: the request is discarded.
  • Reorder: change the positions based on how interesting the questions are.

The first 3 in the queue are pre-connected automatically: their camera and microphone are ready before going on air, so there's no waiting between one question and the next.

06 Put questions on air

Click On air on the first in the queue: the participant enters the video room and asks their question live. When they're done, you move to the next one — the previous one is marked as handled. You see each participant's pre-questions before giving them the air.

07 Record (optional)

Turn on recording from the event panel. The server records the room as an MP4 file that you can then download or send to postPod.

08 End the event

When you click End, the room closes and the event moves to the Ended state. If there was a recording, it's exported automatically to postPod as an editing project.
