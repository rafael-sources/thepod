FEATURES

Every studio tool in detail

A detailed guide to every tool available in the prodPod studio.

01 Pre-join and mic check

Before entering the studio, you pick your camera and microphone from the dropdown menus. You see a preview of your camera and a real-time audio level meter.

The mic check assistant records 10 seconds of your voice, analyzes the level and suggests a gain adjustment. Your adjustment is remembered for that specific microphone.

You can choose the video quality before connecting: Low (360p), Medium (480p) or High (720p).

02 Participant grid

The studio's main view shows every participant in a video grid. The active speaker (whoever is talking) gets a highlighted border. Each tile shows the name, role, mute indicator and audio meter.

Invited participants who haven't connected yet appear dimmed in the grid.

03 Ready, countdown and recording

When the host is ready to record:

  1. Get set: the host clicks “Get set” to signal they're almost ready.
  2. Ready: clicking “Ready” starts a 3-second countdown visible on every screen.
  3. Recording: when it hits zero, recording starts simultaneously in every browser. A red indicator appears with the elapsed time.
  4. Stop: the host clicks “Stop” to end the recording for every participant.

04 Soundboard

A panel of sound pads in the sidebar. Each pad has a name, a shortcut key and a color.

  • Click a pad (or its shortcut key): plays the sound and broadcasts it to every participant.
  • Alt+click: private preview (cue) — only you hear it, it isn't broadcast.
  • Esc or the “Stop all” button: stops every sound that's playing.

While a sound plays, voice audio is lowered automatically (ducking) so the effect comes through clearly. Each pad has its own volume control.

Soundboard triggers are logged with their exact time so postPod can place them on the timeline.

05 Screen sharing

Click the screen button to share your screen, a window or a browser tab. Only one person can share at a time.

The shared screen appears as a large tile above the grid and is recorded as a separate track. You can turn on “screen only” mode, where participants see only your screen, without your camera — handy for demos or presentations.

06 Chat (studio and backstage)

Two chat panels:

  • Studio chat: every participant sees it. For general communication during the recording.
  • Backstage: only the host and co-hosts see it. For private coordination (“we wrap in 2 minutes”, “skip this topic”, “good spot for the cut”).

Messages are saved in the session history.

07 Raise hand

Participants can raise their hand to signal they want to speak or have something to say. A visual indicator appears on their video tile. Click again to lower it. Shortcut: R key.

08 Chapter markers

The host can create markers while recording by clicking the moment button or pressing C.

Planned markers (imported from the prePod rundown) show up in a list — activate them when you reach that point in the recording. Live markers are created on the fly.

Every marker includes a timestamp and becomes a moment when the recording reaches postPod.

09 Practice mode

A rehearsal mode that works exactly like a real session but doesn't record. Use the “Rehearsal” toggle in the top bar.

Use it to test audio levels, practice with the soundboard, check screen sharing and make sure everything works before the real take.

10 RTMP streaming

Stream the session live to YouTube, Twitch or a custom RTMP server:

  1. Click the “Stream” button (only the host sees it).
  2. Choose a preset (YouTube or Twitch) or enter a custom RTMP URL. Paste your stream key.
  3. The stream runs alongside the local recording. You get both: the high-quality local files and the live broadcast.
NOTE

You need at least 5 Mbps of stable upload speed for a stream without dropouts.

11 Diagnostics

A host tool that checks the state of the connection and the room. It shows how many participants are connected and whether there are any discrepancies. Use it if something doesn't seem to be working right.

12 Remove a participant

The host can remove a participant from the session with the kick button. Use it if someone joined by mistake or there's a disruption.

13 Guest experience

Guests get a link to join without needing a thePod account. When they open it, they see the session info and a button to enter.

Before entering, they go through the same pre-join (camera/microphone selection, preview). Once inside, they see the grid, the soundboard and the chat, and they can raise their hand, but they don't have access to the host controls (stop recording, streaming, diagnostics, invitations, kick).

Their audio and video are recorded locally in their browser at the same quality as every other participant.

14 Grid and spotlight views

The grid has two view modes: Grid (every participant at the same size) and Spotlight (the active speaker large, everyone else as thumbnails). Spotlight automatically follows whoever is speaking.

15 Control room (director)

A separate view for a production director, with tools that don't interrupt the participants:

  • Live overlays: text banners, sponsor banners, featured comments, segment lower thirds, countdowns and graphics. They're turned on and off in real time and logged with their timestamp for postPod.
  • Cues to the host: cue buttons — WRAP TOPIC, NEXT TOPIC, PAUSE, FASTER, SLOWER, READ AD or a custom message. The host sees them as a discreet on-screen notice.
  • IFB (talkback): private push-to-talk from the director into the host's ear — the other participants and the recording don't hear it.
  • Force-mute: instantly mute any participant's microphone.
  • Soundboard and chat: the director can fire sounds and write in the backstage.
  • Recording control: start and stop recording from the control room.

16 Sponsor teleprompter

Sponsor segments planned in the prePod rundown generate automatic alerts during the recording: as the ad moment approaches, the host sees a notice with a countdown and the sponsor copy to read. You can dismiss the alert if the timing changed.

17 amaPod panel (Q&A)

If the session has a linked amaPod event, an embedded panel appears with the audience question queue. The host sees the approved questions and puts them on air without leaving the studio.

18 Send to postPod

When the recording ends, the recordings panel shows every track (video + audio per participant, screen recordings). From there:

  • Download ZIP: downloads every track in one compressed file.
  • Send to postPod: creates or updates a project in postPod with every track as a clip, the chapter markers as moments, and the soundboard triggers with their timestamps.
