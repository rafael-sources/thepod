INTEGRATIONS

How prodPod connects with the suite

prodPod is the middle layer: it takes in context from prePod and pushes results to postPod.

01 From prePod

When an episode is sent from prePod, prodPod automatically receives:

  • Guest roster: names, roles and emails, ready to generate invitation links.
  • Soundboard: every sound set up in the podcast, with its name, shortcut and color.
  • Planned markers: the rundown blocks become chapter markers you can activate while recording.

Nothing to set up — everything arrives when the host clicks “Send to prodPod” from prePod.

02 To postPod

When a recording ends, you can send it to postPod. What gets sent:

  • Audio and video tracks from each participant (separate files for precise editing).
  • Screen-share recordings (as additional tracks).
  • Chapter markers: both the planned ones from the rundown and the ones created live during the recording.
  • Soundboard timestamps: every sound trigger is logged with its exact time.
  • Director events and assets: the overlays activated from the control room (banners, sponsors, countdowns, graphics) travel with their timestamps and files so you can play them back in the edit.

In postPod, a project is created (or updated) with all these files as clips ready to add to the timeline.

03 RTMP streaming

Presets included for YouTube and Twitch. All you need to do is paste your stream key. You can also use a custom RTMP URL for other platforms or your own servers.

Streaming runs alongside the local recording — you don't sacrifice quality to broadcast live.

04 Live questions with amaPod

If there's an amaPod event linked to the session, the studio shows an embedded amaPod — Q&A panel: the host sees the queue of approved questions and can put them on air without leaving prodPod. Viewers take part from the event's public page.

05 Production status in prePod

While you record, prePod shows the session status in real time in the episode detail: “recording now” or “recording ready”. That way the planning team knows where production stands without having to ask.
