prodPod

Your remote recording studio. Connect every participant in your podcast over video and audio, and record each track independently, right in each person's browser.

01 What is prodPod?

prodPod is thePod's recording studio. It brings hosts and guests together in a studio-quality video and audio room. Each participant records locally in their browser, which means the recording quality doesn't depend on your internet connection. Even if the video call stutters, everyone's local file comes out clean and in high quality.

When the session ends, all the tracks (separate audio and video per participant) are sent to postPod for editing, along with the chapter markers and the soundboard triggers — all placed on the timeline, ready to work with.

02 What can you do?

  • Record audio and video from each participant separately (independent tracks).
  • Run a mic level assistant before you start.
  • Trigger sound effects and jingles from the soundboard.
  • Mark chapters in real time while recording.
  • Share your screen (recorded as a separate track).
  • Coordinate with your team over the backstage chat (invisible to guests).
  • Stream live to YouTube or Twitch via RTMP, alongside the local recording.
  • Send all the tracks to postPod for editing when you're done.

03 Who is it for?

BASIC

Quick recording

Record a podcast with 1 to 3 people. Enter the studio, start recording, done. Nothing else to set up.

INTERMEDIATE

Production with tools

Use the mic check to dial in your levels, fire soundboard sounds while recording, and mark chapters for editing later.

ADVANCED

Streaming + recording

Stream live to YouTube or Twitch while you record in high quality. Coordinate with your team over the backstage chat without your guests seeing it.
