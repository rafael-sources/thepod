GETTING STARTED

Your first recording, step by step

From opening the session to having your tracks recorded and ready to edit in postPod. If your episode was already sent from prePod, the session will be waiting for you.

01 Open the session

When you enter prodPod you'll see the list of available sessions. Sessions come in from prePod when the host sends an episode to production. Click the session you want to record to open it.

IF YOU DON'T SEE IT

Check with the host that they sent the episode from prePod by clicking “Send to prodPod”. Only participants on the roster can see the session.

02 Set up your camera and microphone

Before entering the studio, you'll land on the pre-join screen. Here you can:

  • Pick your camera from the dropdown menu.
  • Pick your microphone from the dropdown menu.
  • Run the mic check assistant: click “Mic check”, talk normally for 10 seconds, and the system analyzes your audio level. When it's done, it suggests a gain adjustment so your voice sounds clear and even.
TIP

The mic check remembers your settings for each microphone. If you always use the same one, you only need to do it once.

03 Enter the studio

Click “Enter” to go into the studio. You'll see the video grid with every participant who's already connected. Each tile shows the participant's name and role (host, co-host or guest). Whoever is speaking is highlighted with a colored border.

04 Start recording

When everyone is set, the host clicks “Ready”. This starts a 3-second countdown that appears in sync on every participant's screen.

When it hits zero, recording starts automatically in each person's browser. Audio and video are recorded locally, which means the quality doesn't depend on the internet connection — even if the video call stutters, the local file comes out clean.

05 Record your episode

While recording, you can:

  • Fire sounds from the soundboard (click a pad or press keys 1-9).
  • Mark chapters with the chapter button or the C key.
  • Share your screen to show content to the participants (recorded as a separate track).
  • Use the chat to communicate by text (there's a backstage channel just for the host and co-host, invisible to guests).
  • Raise your hand (R key) to signal that you want to jump in.
NOTE

If a guest briefly loses their connection, their local recording keeps running in their browser. Once they reconnect, they'll see everyone else again.

06 Finish and send to postPod

When you're done, the host clicks “Stop recording”. The tracks are saved: one audio file and one video file per participant, plus any screen recordings if there were any.

From the finished-session screen, click “Send to postPod” to create (or update) a project in postPod with:

  • Each track as a clip, ready for the timeline.
  • The chapter markers as moments.
  • The sound triggers as time references.

Head over to the postPod Quickstart to find out what to do with those tracks.
