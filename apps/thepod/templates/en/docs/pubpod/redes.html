WHAT EACH NETWORK ASKS FOR

The rules of each star

Each network has its own conditions, and some change once thePod finishes the Meta, TikTok and Google reviews. Here's what applies today.

01 Connecting, in general

You connect just once, in prePod → Set up podcast → Networks or by tapping a dotted star in pub. The network opens in the browser, you grant the permission to publish on your behalf, and you come back. thePod keeps that permission, never your password; “Disconnect” removes it. If the permission expires, the star goes back to dotted and you connect it again.

02 Instagram and Reels

  • Requires a professional account: Creator or Business. You switch it in the Instagram app: Settings → Account type. A personal account can't be connected.
  • While thePod is under Meta review, only accounts added as testers can publish. If you're a beta tester, write to us with your username and we'll add you; you'll get an invitation to accept in Instagram, under Settings → Websites and apps.
  • Reels go out as a Reel; the full episode goes out as an image (a frame from the video) with the text.
  • The text supports hashtags and mentions. Instagram may take a few seconds to process the video before publishing it; pub waits on its own.

03 TikTok

  • The video isn't published directly: it lands in your TikTok inbox (the “You have a video ready” notification). You open it on your phone, paste the text pub left you with “Copy the text”, and publish it from there. That's TikTok's rule until it reviews the app.
  • Up to 5 pending videos in the inbox per day.
  • While the app is in testing, only accounts added as testers; write to us and we'll tell you how.

04 YouTube and Shorts

  • It's the same connection as prePod's YouTube upload. Shorts is the same channel: pub sends the vertical with #Shorts and YouTube treats it as a Short.
  • Until Google verifies the app, everything uploads as private. Pub marks it “Uploaded as private”; open it on YouTube and switch it to Public.
  • The description includes the chapters if they follow YouTube's rules (the first at 00:00, at least three, 10 s or more apart).

05 Spotify and Apple Podcasts

  • They don't get connected: they read the podcast's feed. They light up when the podcast is set up to publish in prePod.
  • Pub uploads the audio to the feed with the episode notes; Spotify and Apple show it on their own, usually within minutes (Apple sometimes takes longer).
  • The first time, the feed has to be submitted to Spotify and Apple from prePod → Distribution. After that, no more.

06 Facebook

The eighth star. It's in the arc, but for now it shows as “coming very soon”.
