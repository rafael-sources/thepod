SCHEDULE

Now or at its time

Everything you publish can go out right away or be scheduled. Scheduled posts are handled by thePod on the server: it doesn't need the app open or your Mac on.

01 How to schedule

  1. Pick what to publish and write the texts, same as for publishing now.
  2. Tap Schedule instead of Now and pick a date and time (in your local time). A date that's already passed isn't accepted.
  3. Tap Export and schedule. The app exports and uploads the file right then, so that at the set time all that's left is to publish.

In Posts it shows as Scheduled, with the time. Each destination is a separate post: you can cancel the TikTok one and keep the Reels one.

02 If you close the app

Once the app has finished uploading the file (the bar reaches 100 %), you can close it or shut down your Mac: the post still goes out at its time. If you close before the upload finishes, the post is left without a file and pub lets you know when you come back, with Retry.

03 Cancel and retry

  • Cancel: in the list, as long as it says Scheduled. Once it starts publishing it can't be cancelled; if it went out, delete it on the network.
  • Retry: on one that says Not published. “View details…” gives the reason (expired permission, text too long, the network didn't respond). If the reason is the permission, connect the account again and retry.
  • If thePod was restarting right at that time, the post goes out as soon as it's back; it isn't lost.

04 A video from your computer

The From your computer card is for publishing a video that didn't come out of post: a vertical you edited in another program, for example. The same stars light up as for a reel (Shorts, Reels, TikTok). Formats: MP4 or MOV, in 9:16 vertical so the networks show it as a reel.

05 Limits to keep in mind

Instagram and ReelsUp to 2,200 charactersReel from 3 s to 15 min, 9:16

If a text goes over, pub warns you before publishing. The “Always at the end” signature counts toward the limit.
