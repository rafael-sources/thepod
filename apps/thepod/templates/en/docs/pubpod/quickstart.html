QUICKSTART · 5 MINUTES

Your first post

From a reel in post to your Instagram, Shorts or TikTok. You need thePod for Mac with your account connected and an episode with at least one reel.

01 Connect a network

In the app, at the top, tap pre → your podcast → Set up podcast → Networks. Tap Connect next to YouTube, Instagram or TikTok. The browser opens, you grant the permission, and back in the app your username appears next to the account. You can also do it by tapping a dotted star in pub.

Instagram requires a professional account (Creator or Business). If yours is personal, in the Instagram app: Settings → Account type → Switch to professional account. It's free and takes a minute.

02 Pick what to publish

Tap pub. On the left, your episodes; pick one. The cards with thumbnails appear: Full episode, each reel and From your computer. Tap a reel.

Up top, in the arc, the stars that belong to a reel light up: Shorts, Reels and TikTok. If one is dotted, tap it to connect it. If you don't want one of the lit ones in this post, open it by its name and tap Remove from this post.

03 Write the text

Below there's one tab per destination. Write the text for each one: the Short's, the Instagram one with its hashtags, the one you'll paste into TikTok. If you have a signature in Always at the end for that network, it's added automatically at the bottom; don't repeat it.

04 Publish

Leave Now and tap Export and publish. The app exports the reel if needed (you'll see the percentage), uploads it, and each star tells you how it went. You can close the app: thePod finishes on its own.

  • Shorts: stays Uploaded as private until Google verifies the app. Open it on YouTube and switch it to Public.
  • Reels: shows as Published, with the link.
  • TikTok: shows as In your TikTok inbox. Open TikTok on your phone, paste the text you copied and publish it from there.

05 Next time, schedule it

Same path, but instead of Now tap Schedule, pick a date and time and Export and schedule. You'll see it in Posts as Scheduled, with Cancel until it starts. More in Schedule.
