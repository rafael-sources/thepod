pubPod

Where what comes out of post gets published. Eight destinations. You pick the full episode or a reel, write the text for each network, and Export and publish: now, or at whatever time you say. It's the white section of thePod for Mac.

01 What is it?

pubPod isn't a separate app: it's the pub section of thePod for Mac, alongside pre, prod, ama and post. It takes what post exported (or exports it on the spot) and uploads it to your networks with the permission you granted once in prePod. If you close the app, thePod keeps going on its own: a scheduled post still goes out at its time.

The eight stars are the eight destinations: Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube, Instagram, Shorts, Reels, TikTok and Facebook (coming very soon). A filled star is connected; a dotted one isn't.

02 What can you do?

EPISODE

The full episode

The 16:9 video to YouTube, the audio to the feed (Spotify and Apple Podcasts read it on their own) and the cover, a frame from the video with text, to Instagram.

REELS

Each reel

The 9:16 vertical, exactly as it looks in the editor, to Shorts, Reels and TikTok. Also a video you already have on your computer.

TEXT

One text per destination

One tab per network: the YouTube description, the Short's text, the Instagram one with hashtags, the episode notes, the text to paste into TikTok.

SIGNATURE

Always at the end

A signature or hashtags per destination that get added automatically at the bottom of every post. You write it once, in the star's panel.

WHEN

Now or scheduled

Right away, or with a date and time. Scheduled posts go out even if the app is closed, and can be cancelled until they start.

TRACKING

Posts

The list of what went out and what's coming, with its status and its link: Scheduled, Publishing, Published, Uploaded as private, In your inbox, Not published.

03 How it fits with the rest

  • prePod → Networks connects the accounts, just once. It doesn't publish.
  • post edits and marks the reels. Each reel also has a ✈️ button to publish it without leaving the editor.
  • pub picks, writes and publishes, and keeps the record.
No passwords are stored. What you give when connecting a network is only the permission to publish on your behalf, and it's removed with “Disconnect”. What thePod does with each network is in Privacy.
