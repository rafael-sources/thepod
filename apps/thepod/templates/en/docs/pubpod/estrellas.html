THE STARS

Eight destinations

At the top of pub, in an arc: Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube, Instagram, Shorts, Reels, TikTok and Facebook. A filled star is connected; a dotted one with “Connect” isn't. When you pick what to publish, the ones that apply light up.

01 The arc

Filled: connected · Dotted: tap to connect · Pulsing: publishing
  • The ends dip down and the middle rises. The outer ones are the first to light up when you open pub.
  • A star glows when it lights up and pulses while something is being published through it.
  • Tapping a dotted star opens the browser to connect that account. Tapping the name of a lit one opens its panel.

02 What lights up what

You pickLights upWhat gets uploaded
Full episodeYouTube · Spotify · Apple Podcasts · InstagramThe 16:9 video to YouTube; the audio to the feed, with the episode notes; the cover chosen in post → Cover (or a frame from the video) with text to Instagram.
ReelShorts · Reels · TikTokThe 9:16 vertical exactly as it looks in the editor, with the reel's subtitles, music and lower thirds.
From your computerShorts · Reels · TikTokA video you already have, without going through the editor.
Facebook—Coming very soon.

Spotify and Apple Podcasts are two stars but a single connection: the podcast feed. Both read the same thing.

03 Each star's panel

Tap a star's name, or press and hold it. Its panel opens:

  • The account: connected username, “View profile”, and Disconnect or Switch.
  • Always at the end: a signature or hashtags that get added automatically at the bottom of every text you publish to that destination. You write it once and it applies to every post that follows.
  • Latest: the last thing that went out through there, with its status and its link.
  • Include / Remove from this post: to leave a star out this time without disconnecting it.

04 The text for each destination

Below the cards, one tab per lit star. Each network reads its own text:

  • YouTube: the description (chapters go in automatically if they follow YouTube's rules).
  • Shorts: the Short's text; #Shorts is added to it.
  • Instagram and Reels: the text with your hashtags.
  • Spotify and Apple Podcasts: the episode notes, the same for both.
  • TikTok: the text you'll paste when publishing from your phone; “Copy the text” gets it ready.

05 Posts

At the bottom, the list of what went out and what's coming, with the time and status of each one:

StatusWhat it means
ScheduledGoes out at the time you chose. Can be cancelled until it starts.
Publishing…Uploading now; the star pulses.
PublishedDone, with the link.
Uploaded as privateIt's on YouTube but not public yet: open it and switch it to Public.
In your TikTok inboxThe video reached TikTok; finish publishing it from your phone.
Not publishedSomething failed; “View details…” says what, and “Retry” tries again.
CancelledYou cancelled it before it went out.
