FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions about editing in postPod.

Are my original files modified?

No, never. postPod is 100% non-destructive. Every cut, effect and operation is saved as an instruction. The original files stay untouched. When you export, a new file is generated by applying all the instructions.

Can I undo an operation?

Yes. Select the moment, go to the operations panel and delete or edit the operation you want to change. Since editing is non-destructive, there's no limit to how much you can change.

How do I make a vertical clip for TikTok?

It depends on your material:

  • A single camera (regular 16:9 video): apply the To vertical (9:16) operation on a moment. Adjust the horizontal center to frame the subject.
  • Composite video (two or more cameras side by side in the same file): apply Vertical split. Set the number of cameras and the center of each panel.

In both cases, click the moment's export button to render just that section with the TikTok / Reels vertical preset.

How do I export only part of the video?

You have two options:

  1. Manual selection: drag across the timeline ruler to create a range (yellow band). Open Export and check “Export selected range only”.
  2. From a moment: click the export button (arrow icon) on any moment in the list. The dialog opens with the moment's range already loaded.
Are captions automatic?

Yes. In the files panel, click the captions button on a video or audio file, pick the language and the model, and the system generates word-by-word subtitles. The process runs in the background — you can keep editing while they're generated.

What is professional audio?

It's optional processing applied when you export. It includes: background noise reduction, EQ for voice clarity, compression for even volume, and normalization to -16 LUFS (broadcast standard). It only affects the voice — background music passes through unchanged.

Relative or absolute moments: which should I use?

Use relative (the default) if you want the moment to move when you add or remove clips before it. Use absolute if you need the moment to stay at a fixed time no matter what changes in the timeline. In most cases, relative is the right choice.

How does the transcript editor work?

Open the editor and you'll see the text of your clips' audio. If you delete a fragment of text, that fragment of audio/video is cut from the export. It's the most intuitive way to remove filler words, long pauses or sections you don't want. You can turn on filler word highlighting to detect them automatically and remove them in bulk.

Can I add background music?

Yes. Upload the audio file to the project and add it to the audio lane (the parallel track below the timeline, not the main timeline). Adjust the volume and the start/end points. The music will play underneath the main content.

The export takes a long time, is that normal?

It depends on the video's length and resolution. A 1-hour video at 1080p can take several minutes. If you turned on “professional audio”, add extra time for the processing. You can keep working while the export runs in the background.

Can I export the same project several times with different presets?

Yes. You can export as many times as you want, with different presets and options. Each export is independent. The export history appears in the export dialog.

What file formats can I upload?
  • Video: MP4, MOV, WebM
  • Audio: MP3, WAV, M4A
  • Image: JPG, PNG, GIF

The maximum size per file is 2 GB.

Is the audio lane louder than the voice?

Adjust the clip's volume in the audio lane. For background music under voice, somewhere between 15% and 25% is typical. If you have professional audio on, make sure the music is already low in the mix before exporting.
