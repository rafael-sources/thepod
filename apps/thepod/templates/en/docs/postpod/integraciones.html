INTEGRATIONS

How postPod connects with the suite

postPod is the last layer: it receives the material from prodPod and produces the final files ready to publish.

01 From prodPod

When a recording in prodPod is sent to postPod, a project is created (or updated) with:

  • Audio and video tracks for each participant as separate files, ready to add to the timeline.
  • Screen share recordings as additional tracks.
  • Chapter markers: both the ones planned in the prePod rundown and the ones created live during the recording. They arrive as moments on the timeline.
  • Soundboard timestamps: every sound trigger with its exact time, so you can find them while editing.

You don't need to upload anything by hand — everything arrives in the project automatically.

02 From prePod

If the episode was planned in prePod and recorded in prodPod, the postPod project stays linked to the original episode. When you send the episode from prePod, postPod automatically imports and downloads:

  • Podcast branding: logo, background and cover, ready for overlays and exports.
  • Sponsor logos for the episode, linked to their rundown segments.
  • Soundboard sounds for the podcast, with their metadata (name, shortcut, color) in a manifest.
  • Guest photos, tied to each participant for banners and credits.
  • Rundown with planned offsets: each block arrives with the minute it was scheduled for, so you can compare it with what happened in the recording.

If you send the episode again from prePod, the existing project is refreshed: sponsors, sounds, guests and branding are updated without touching your edit. All this material also travels inside the bundle when you export or import the project between teams.

03 Captions with Whisper

postPod uses Whisper (an AI model) to generate word-by-word subtitles in multiple languages. Captions can:

  • Show live during playback (CC button in the player).
  • Be burned into the video on export (always visible, no need for a player that supports SRT).
  • Be exported as a standalone SRT for platforms that accept subtitle files.

05 Chapter notes

When you export with the “Generate notes” option, postPod creates a .txt file with:

  • Chapters in YouTube format (00:00 Title) ready to paste into the video description.
  • Credits and episode metadata.

Compatible with Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube and other directories that support chapters.

06 Professional audio

A processing option at export time that applies:

  • Background noise reduction.
  • Voice EQ (low-frequency cut, clarity in the upper mids).
  • Compression for a more even volume.
  • Normalization to -16 LUFS (broadcast standard).

It only affects the voice track — background music passes through unchanged.
