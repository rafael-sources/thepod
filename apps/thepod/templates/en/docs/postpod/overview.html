postPod

A non-destructive video and audio editor for podcast and videocast post-production. Your original files are never modified — every cut, effect and operation is saved as an instruction and applied when you export.

01 What is postPod?

postPod is a video and audio editor built for podcast and videocast post-production. Its core principle is non-destructive editing: it never touches your original files. Everything you do — cuts, zooms, color effects, subtitles, audio mixes, reframing to vertical — is saved as instructions in the project. When you export, a new file is generated by applying all those instructions in a single pass.

That means you can experiment freely: add operations, remove them, change them, with no risk of losing anything. And you can generate multiple versions of the same episode (YouTube 16:9, TikTok 9:16, podcast MP3) without redoing the work.

02 What can you do?

  • Build a timeline with video and audio clips, then reorder and trim them.
  • Mark moments and apply operations: zoom, color effects, subtitles, audio mix, vertical crop for TikTok, animated text stickers.
  • Edit the transcript visually: deleting text removes the matching audio/video.
  • Generate captions (word-by-word subtitles) with Whisper in multiple languages.
  • Add background music in the audio lane.
  • Export to YouTube, TikTok/Reels, Instagram, WhatsApp, or podcast (audio only), with professional audio enhancement options and automatic chapter notes.

03 Who is it for?

BASIC

Quick editing

Build the timeline with clips, trim the parts you don't need, and export. The essentials for publishing an edited episode.

INTERMEDIATE

Editing with effects

Use moments and operations to add zooms, effects and subtitles. Edit the transcript to cut filler words. Generate captions with Whisper.

ADVANCED

Professional multi-format

Create vertical clips for TikTok, add animated text overlays, use the audio lane for music, and turn on professional audio when exporting.
