KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS

Hotkeys and mouse gestures

Shortcuts to edit efficiently in postPod without relying on the mouse.

01 Player and navigation

KeyAction
Espacio
 Play / pause
←
 Go back 5 seconds
→
 Go forward 5 seconds
Shift + ←
 Go back 1 frame (1/30 s)
Shift + →
 Go forward 1 frame (1/30 s)
M
 Capture a moment at the current time
+
 Zoom in the timeline 10%
-
 Zoom out the timeline 10%
Z
 Zoom to the selected range
Esc
 Clear the range selection / reset zoom

02 Timeline (mouse gestures)

GestureAction
Click the ruler
 Move the playhead to that point
Drag across the ruler
 Select a range (yellow band)
Drag the playhead (red cursor)
 Scrub frame by frame
Double-click a clip
 Open the clip editor
Click a moment marker
 Select that moment and jump to it

03 Transcript editor

ActionResult
Click a word
 The player jumps to that time
Select text and delete (Delete / Backspace)
 Cuts that section of the audio/video
Ctrl / Cmd + Z
 Undo the last cut
Esc
 Clear selection
NOTE

Keyboard shortcuts only work when your cursor isn't inside a text field.
