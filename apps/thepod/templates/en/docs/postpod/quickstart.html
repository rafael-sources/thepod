GETTING STARTED

Your first edit, step by step.

A quick guide to go from raw files to an exported episode. If your project comes from prodPod, the files and markers are already included — you can skip to step 3.

01 Open the project

Projects arrive from prodPod (with the recordings and markers already included), or you can create one manually from the project list.

The main interface has: the video player at the top, the timeline in the center, the files panel on the left, moments below the timeline, and the clips/operations panel on the right.

02 Upload files (if needed)

If your project comes from prodPod, the files are already there. If not, go to the Files panel (left), click upload and pick your files. Supported formats: MP4, MOV, WebM (video), MP3, WAV, M4A (audio), JPG, PNG, GIF (image). Maximum size per file: 2 GB.

03 Add clips to the timeline

In the files panel, click a video or audio file to add it to the timeline. Clips show up in the timeline bar in sequence. You can reorder them by dragging in the Clips panel (right) using the 6-dot handle.

To trim a clip, click it in the Clips panel and adjust the start (IN) and end (OUT) points.

04 Play and navigate

  • Space — play / pause.
  • ← → — jump 5 seconds.
  • Shift + ← → — jump 1 second.
  • Click the timeline ruler — move the playhead to that point.
  • Drag the playhead (red cursor) — scrub frame by frame.

05 Mark moments

Press M to capture a moment at the current time. Give it a descriptive label (double-click the name to edit it). Moments are the anchor points where you can apply operations.

If your project comes from prodPod, the chapter markers from the recording already show up as moments in the timeline.

06 Apply operations

Select a moment in the list. In the Operations panel (right), add an operation: zoom, color effect, subtitle, audio mix, vertical crop for TikTok, animated text, silence removal, and more. The player shows the effect in real time.

Each moment has quick-action buttons: jump to that point in the timeline, preview the effect, export just that moment, or delete it.

07 Edit the transcript (optional)

Open the Transcript Editor from the toolbar. You'll see the text of your clips' audio. If you delete a fragment of text, that fragment of audio/video is cut from the export. It's the most intuitive way to remove filler words or unwanted sections.

  • Click a word — the player jumps to that point.
  • Select text and press Delete — creates a cut (non-destructive, reversible).
  • Turn on “Highlight filler words” to automatically detect “eh”, “um”, “este”, “o sea” and similar.
  • Use “Remove filler words in bulk” to cut them all at once.
REQUIREMENT

To use the transcript editor, generate captions for the file first (step 8).

08 Generate captions (optional)

In the Files panel, click the captions button on a video or audio file. Pick the language (Spanish, English, Portuguese, etc.) and the model. Whisper generates word-by-word subtitles in the background — you can keep editing while they're generated.

09 Export

Click Export in the toolbar. Choose a preset:

  • YouTube — 1080p or 4K, 30 or 60 fps.
  • TikTok / Reels — 1080x1920 vertical.
  • Instagram — 1080x1080 square.
  • WhatsApp — 720p compressed.
  • Podcast — MP3 or M4A (audio only).

Extra options when exporting:

  • Burn captions — embeds the subtitles into the video.
  • Generate notes — creates YouTube-style chapters and credits.
  • Professional audio — noise reduction, equalization, compression and normalization.
  • Export range only — if you have a range selected, exports just that portion.

The render runs in the background with a progress bar. When it finishes, download the file or play it right away.

NON-DESTRUCTIVE

Everything is non-destructive: your original files are never modified. You can experiment freely with operations and undo them without losing anything. And you can export the same project several times with different presets.
