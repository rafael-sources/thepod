INTEGRATIONS

How the app connects with the suite

It uses the same account as the rest of thePod. It brings the project from postPod, everything about the episode from prePod, and publishes with what prePod already has set up.

01 One account

The username and password are your thePod ones. The app signs in to prePod and postPod with those credentials and only sees what your account can see on the web (your projects, or all of them if you're an administrator). Your account is stored on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/postPod/cuenta.dat (not in the keychain, so macOS doesn't ask for your password on every update).

02 The pre section is prePod

What you do in pre is saved straight to prePod, instantly: episodes, script, rundown, guests, ideas, templates, branding, team, sponsors, soundboard, your page and distribution. You see the same thing on the web and in the app, and emails to guests go out from prePod.

The script's teleprompter sections (who reads it, when, speed, size, chaining) are also saved in prePod, and it's prePod that hands them to the prodPod room, whether the room is on the Mac or on the web.

03 The prod section is prodPod

The app's room joins the same room as prodPod web: hosts and director from the Mac or the web, mixed; guests via the web. What the Mac adds: the recording happens on your Mac in 1080p (or 720p if the connection can't keep up) and uploads in chunks while you record, so if the connection drops nothing is lost. The lower thirds the director launches live, the sponsors marked at their moment, the soundboard effects and the moments arrive in post already placed at their second.

04 From postPod

Bring to the Mac downloads the project from postPod (the same thing the web's export button does) and opens it. From there, the app uses:

  • Tracks for each participant (video and audio), with their time offset.
  • Participants and their names.
  • Transcripts already made, if they exist, and the cuts from the transcript editor.
  • Moments, which become reels.
  • Subtitle style set up on the web.
  • Effects already placed on the audio line, at their exact moment, and the podcast's soundboard to add more.
  • Guest photos, which show next to their name in the video.
  • The director's lower thirds, with their exact second, marked “from the director”.
ONE WAY

The app brings projects in, but doesn't send the edit back to postPod web. If you change something on the web (for example, mark more moments), use Bring again.

05 From prePod

If the episode was planned in prePod, the package brings and the app uses:

  • Episode data: number, title, description, podcast summary and links. They go into the metadata and the export notes.
  • Branding: the logo as a watermark and the podcast background in the Studio layout.
  • Sponsors with their logo: they appear in the video at the script block they belong to (the real minute if prodPod marked it, otherwise the planned one).
  • Script: visible in Your recording and used for the chapters.

06 Publishing with prePod

The app stores no credentials for any network: it publishes through prePod, where accounts are connected once (Podcast settings → Networks). In the app, the pub section is the one that publishes.
