QUICKSTART

Your first steps on the Mac.

Install, connect your account, plan an episode in pre, record it in prod and finish it in post.

01 Install the app

Download it from Downloads (while signed in to thePod), drag thePod to your Applications folder and open it. There's nothing else to install.

FIRST TIME

The app is signed and notarized by Apple: it opens without security warnings. The first time, it asks for permission to use the camera and microphone, and to use your saved password: choose Always Allow so it doesn't ask again.

02 Connect your account

Open Settings (⌘ ,) and, under Your prePod / postPod account, type your username and password. It's the same account you use on the web. Tap Save and test: it should tell you how many podcasts and projects your account can see. You type it once: the app never asks again, not even when it updates.

Your account is stored on your Mac (not in the keychain, so macOS doesn't ask for your password on every update).

03 Plan the episode (pre)

Tap pre next to “thePod”. You'll see your episodes in columns by status; drag them from one column to the next as they move along. With New episode you pick the title and the template, and the episode is born with its rundown and script.

When you open an episode you get: Script, Rundown, Guests (with invitation and prep email), Ideas and Publishing. Everything saves itself as you type.

In the script, a section marked as Teleprompter shows up in the room for the person you choose, at the minute and second you set (or right after the previous one). That way nobody has to remember the sponsor's lines.

04 Record (prod)

Tap prod or, from the episode, Go record. Check your camera, microphone and headphones, run the connection test and enter the room. Everyone else joins from the Mac or from the web; guests always via the web. Once everyone is ready, countdown and record: each person records on their own Mac and the recording uploads in chunks while they talk. When you're done, Send to post.

05 Bring the recording (post)

Tap post. On the home screen, the In postPod web section lists the projects your account can see. Tap Bring to the Mac: postPod builds the package (it can take a few minutes for long episodes), the app downloads it showing progress and imports it on its own.

No connection to the web? You can also drag into the window the package you download from postPod, a folder with each person's tracks, or loose video files.

TIP

Some videos get prepared once, on import. After that, everything is fast.

06 Prepare

Recording: check that everyone is there and give each person a name (they show up in the video). Clean sound: one button makes studio sound, removes long silences and filler words, and writes the transcript on your Mac (the first time takes a few minutes). Nothing is actually deleted: Undo cuts brings everything back. Subtitles: pick a style and fix it sentence by sentence. Sensitive words: finds swear words and delicate topics; bleep on top, softened subtitle, or both.

07 Build

Look: how the cameras are arranged, virtual background, color, podcast branding, intro and outro. Texts, stickers and b-roll, Music and effects (from the soundboard, your own files or synthetic voice), news-style Lower thirds and sponsors at their moment. Everything moves with the mouse over the preview and with h:mm:ss time fields.

If someone coughs or shouts, drag over their row in the timeline and tap Mute only X or −6 dB on X: the video doesn't get shorter. With ⌘ Z you undo anything.

08 Reels

Every moment you marked in postPod, or with Mark moment below the video (the M key, or I and O), becomes a vertical reel with subtitles. Adjust the length (30, 60 or 90 s), change its layout, subtitles, music, b-roll or lower thirds in their tabs, pick the ones you want and tap Export.

08b Pick the cover

In Cover, tap Find the best frames: the app scans the video and shows you, in a carousel, the seconds where everyone is smiling, eyes open and in focus. Click one and it becomes the episode image: the one pub sends to Instagram and the thumbnail for YouTube. If you'd rather use another moment, pause the episode there and tap Capture here.

09 Export

Choose where you're going to publish it —YouTube (16:9), Reels/TikTok/Shorts (9:16), Instagram square (1:1) or audio only (MP3)— and whether it goes as H.264 (compatible) or HEVC (lightweight). The final loudness is set automatically to −16 LUFS. The files land in the project's Exports folder, along with a notes text file (chapters, guests, sponsors and links). If you want to finish the mix in another program, Separate tracks (WAV).

10 Publish

Open pub (at the top, next to post). Pick the episode, then what you're publishing: the full episode, a reel or a video from your computer. The stars for the matching destinations light up —YouTube, Spotify and Apple Podcasts plus the cover to Instagram for the episode; Shorts, Reels and TikTok for a reel—; turn off the ones you don't want, write the text for each one, and tap Export and publish (or Schedule with a date and time). If you haven't exported yet, the app exports first, and keeps going on its own even if you close it. A dotted star is an account that isn't connected: tap it and grant permission in the browser. You can also publish a reel straight from its card in post, with ✈️.
