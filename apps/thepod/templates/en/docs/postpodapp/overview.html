thePod for Mac

The whole suite in one native app for your Mac: pre to plan, prod to record with other people, ama for questions from your audience, post to edit and pub to publish. Made for people who have never edited a video.

01 What is thePod for Mac?

It's the same suite you use on the web, as an app for Macs with Apple silicon. At the top, next to the name, you pick a section: pre (the same as prePod), prod (the prodPod room, recording on your Mac), ama (amaPod), post, the biggest part: it takes the recording and gets it ready for YouTube, Spotify, reels and TikTok, and pub: eight stars, one destination each, where you publish what came out of post, right away or on a schedule.

All the heavy lifting happens on your Mac: transcribing, separating people from the background, mixing, rendering. It never sends audio or video to any server, except when you upload a recording to prodPod or tap Publish. It carries its own ffmpeg inside: nothing to install.

02 What can you do?

In post (edit and publish), in three blocks:

  • Prepare: review the recording, clean sound (studio sound, no silences, no filler words, equalizer, even voice, less hiss), subtitles sentence by sentence and sensitive words (a bleep on top and a softened subtitle).
  • Build: look (camera layouts, virtual background, color, branding, intro and outro), texts, stickers and b-roll, music and effects (with synthetic voice), news-style lower thirds and sponsors at their moment.
  • Mix: waveforms, solo and mute, meters, drawn volume per person, −16 LUFS on export and separate WAV stems.
  • Deliver: reels with their own layout, subtitles, music, b-roll and lower thirds; the episode cover, found by searching for the seconds where everyone is smiling; export for YouTube, reels, square or MP3; publish a reel to social networks from its card.

In pre: board, calendar, script (with teleprompter sections: who reads it, when, chained), rundown, guests, ideas, templates, page, team and podcast settings.

In prod: the recording room with countdown, rehearsal, recording on the Mac with chunked upload, automatic teleprompter, soundboard, sponsors, auto-mute, and the director's control room (lower thirds, cues, prompter, whisper in someone's ear).

In ama: events, question queue, on air, moderated chat and recording.

In pub: the full episode to YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts and the cover to Instagram; reels to Shorts, Reels and TikTok. You choose what, write the text for each destination, and “Export and publish”, now or at a set time. Accounts are connected once, in pre → Networks.

03 How it updates

On launch, the app checks for a new version; you can also check in Settings → Check now. It downloads, verifies the file and swaps it in when you relaunch. If anything fails, the previous version stays intact.

SIGNED AND NOTARIZED BY APPLE

Since 0.29.2 the app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple: it installs and opens without security warnings. If you're coming from an earlier version, the first time you open it it asks again for camera and microphone permission, because the signature changed; after that it won't happen again.

05 Requirements

  • A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).
  • macOS 15 or later.
  • Your thePod account.
  • Internet the first time you transcribe (it downloads the model once), to bring and upload recordings, and to publish.
