KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS

Keys and gestures in the editor

To move around the episode, mark reels and undo without letting go of the keyboard. They work in post when you're not typing in a text field.

01 Move and play

KeyAction

Play / pause

Import recording
⌘ + ,
Settings

The big clock above the video has a keyboard button: type a time as h:mm:ss and the video jumps there. Every time field in the app (music, texts, lower thirds, reels) accepts that same format.

02 Mark moments and reels

KeyAction
M
Mark moment Creates a 60 s reel from where the video is.
I
Mark the start of a reel at the clock's time.
O
Mark the end and create the reel If you're viewing a reel, O adjusts its end.

03 Edit and undo

KeyAction
⌘ + Z
Undo
⇧ + ⌘ + Z
Redo
Suprimir
Remove the selected volume point on the yellow line in the timeline.

Undo covers any change to the episode: cuts, deletions, texts, lower thirds, reels, layout, volume points. It keeps up to 120 steps; consecutive changes (dragging a control) count as one. Inside a text field, ⌘ Z undoes what you typed, not the episode.

04 With the mouse, in the timeline

GestureWhat it does
ClickTakes the video to that point.
Drag over the ruler or a rowMarks a stretch. Listen, Mute only X, −6 dB on X and Cut from everyone appear.
Double-click over a personAdds a volume point on the yellow line.
Drag a pointUp and down changes the volume (−60 to +12 dB, in 1 dB steps; the value shows while you drag), sideways moves it in time.
S / M next to the nameSolo (only that person is heard) and Mute.
Click a person's name or a music trackOpens its panel below the video.
Drag over the previewMoves texts, stickers, images and the subtitles box; the corner resizes.

05 In Studio

The ones above also work in Studio (⌘E audio, ⇧⌘E video). On top of that, and under the window's “?” button:

KeyAction
⇧ + ⌘ + B
A/B Hear the voices with no chain while playing.
1 2 3 4
Multicam Cut to that camera at the current second (Video tab).
?
The list of shortcuts
Right-click on the Cameras trackPin a camera or a template at that second; drag its edges to stretch the segment.
Diamond next to a controlSet or remove a keyframe at this second.
Double-click on an automation laneDelete that point.
⌥ + click on a faderReset that fader to 0 dB.

06 In the room (prod)

The room keys are the same as in prodPod web. To whisper in someone's ear from the control room, press and hold that person's button.
